Services

What we handle

Scorpions, ants, cockroaches, earwigs, spiders, fruit flies, ticks and fleas — plus rats, mice and bats. If it’s in your home and shouldn’t be, call us.

Ants

Carpenter ants can form huge colonies and excavate tunnels in wood, compromising the framing structure of your home. Fire ants bite and sting. We keep them away with a monthly service.

Spiders

Southern California is home to many spiders — including the black widow, whose bite is poisonous and can be lethal, and the desert recluse, whose bite can leave severe lesions.

Scorpions

Scorpions in and around the home, handled by professionals — along with cockroaches, earwigs and fruit flies.

Rats & Mice

Rats and mice invade attics and sub-areas in search of food and water. We specialize in trapping, removing, cleaning, and sealing them out.

Bats

Bats pose real health risks and can fit through a hole the size of a dime. We get them out of your home, then seal every entry point so they cannot move back in.

Fleas & Ticks

Fleas and ticks in the home and yard — treated as part of your regular service so they don’t come back.

On the job

We don’t just spray — we solve it

Rodents are dirty creatures — the deer mouse, for example, has tested positive for Hantavirus, and there’s no cure for Hantavirus. That’s why we trap, remove, clean and seal: so the problem is gone, not just hidden.
